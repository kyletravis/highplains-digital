Offerings

Honest work. Not a catalog.

Four things we actually do when we show up. Scoped to the problem. Priced as work, not as a subscription.

01

Find the real problem

Discovery in the workflow, not in a conference room far from the data. Sit with the people who do the work. Watch what actually happens. Name the constraint that blocks value — not the one that photographs well.

02

Prototype in their environment

Build where the data already lives. Same systems, same constraints, same operators. A prototype that only works in a lab is still a demo. If it cannot run in their stack, it is not finished.

03

Harden for production

Observability, ownership, and the unglamorous work that lets it run on a Tuesday when nobody is watching. Then we hand it off. Success is a system the team can operate without us hovering.

04

Executive-to-engineer translation

Stakeholder-ready artifacts that tell the truth: what the system does, what it does not, who owns it, and what it takes to keep it running. Not a product. The communication layer that keeps a technical team and a leadership team on the same page.

How we engage

Scoped to the problem. Then done.

We do not sell seats, tiers, or a monthly AI platform. An engagement starts with the stuck place, ends when the team can run what we built, and does not quietly turn into staff-aug.

What you get

A working slice in your environment, ownership named, and enough documentation that the next Tuesday does not require us on the call.

What you do not get

A retainer that never ends, a slide deck in place of a system, or a productized plan with a price on a pricing page.
