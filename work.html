The shapes

Where teams get stuck.

These are patterns, not logos. If one of them is the room you are in, that is the conversation.

01

Demo-stuck AI

A promising notebook, a chatbot that impressed a room, a vendor proof-of-concept that never left the sandbox. Leadership wants it in the business. The team cannot get it past the demo. We sit with the workflow and close that gap.

02

Productionizing RAG and agents

Retrieval, agents, and copilots that have to run on real data platforms — warehouses, lakes, operational systems — not a clean sample set. Same access patterns, same constraints, same operators. If it cannot live there, it is still a demo.

03

Technical communication drag

The system is further along than the story. Docs, architecture notes, and stakeholder artifacts lag the work. Engineers and executives are not looking at the same picture. We write the layer that tells the truth: what it does, what it does not, who owns it.

04

Fortune 100 environments

Telecom, financial services, automotive, media. Large-scale data and AI that had to survive access reviews, change windows, and a Tuesday when nobody is watching. We do not name the logos. The environments are the point.

Named work

Case studies when we can share them.

Most of this work sits under NDA, inside platforms we do not own, for companies that do not want their name on a studio site. We will not invent a portfolio to fill the gap.

What we will put here

Named engagements, with permission, when the work can be described without dressing it up. Until then, the problem shapes above are the honest record.

What we will not put here

Invented clients, borrowed logos, anonymous quotes, or metrics we cannot stand behind. A blank case-study slot is better than a fake one.
